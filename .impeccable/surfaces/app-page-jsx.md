## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a wafer inspection map: data arrives as a scatter of fail, marginal and pass bins and resolves into a decision. It refuses the analyst-portfolio default of a headline, a stat row and a grid of equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Silicon-grey ground with ink-navy type; colour is data, never decoration: pass green, marginal amber, fail red as bin codes; a wafer's circular die grid, notch and edge exclusion ring; numbers in tabular figures. Thin hairline rules like an inspection report, no gradients, no glass.

STORY: The visitor sees a messy wafer turn clean while reading "I turn messy data into decisions", learns he is entry level with real business exposure, then reads four projects as inspection reports (fail = the problem, amber = what I did, green = the result) and decides to email him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 45%: name in the nav, H1 at display scale, one line of positioning, Email and LinkedIn as the primary actions. Right 55%: a large wafer (about 560px) whose four quadrants are the four projects, labelled on hover and focus, clickable to their sections; the die colours sort from messy to clean once.
